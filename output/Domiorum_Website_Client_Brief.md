# Domiorum Architects | Website advancement brief

Client questions and next-stage approval | 4 October 2026


## Website direction & approval

### Introduction

We have reviewed the identity presentation, website strategy and visual boards for Domiorum Architects. The proposed website will introduce the practice, demonstrate its work and encourage relevant project enquiries. Please answer the questions below, mark anything still undecided, and identify who can provide each missing asset.

### Working direction to confirm

A calm, precise and welcoming architecture website, with generous spacing, natural-light imagery and clear project stories. Proposed navigation: Home, Projects, Studio, Services and Contact, with individual project pages. Primary action: Discuss your project. Secondary action: View projects. These are proposals for your approval.

### 1. What should the website achieve?

Please rank your priorities: new residential enquiries, renovation/interior enquiries, developer partnerships, portfolio credibility or another goal. What would make the first three months after launch successful?

### 2. Who and where should we target?

Which client group matters most? Which towns, regions or countries do you serve? Are there project types, sizes or budget levels you want to attract or exclude?

### 3. Can we approve the latest visual direction?

The newer strategy and both boards use midnight navy #08182E, warm gold #E1BF77, warm ivory #F6F3ED and graphite #29313A. The earlier identity PDF uses different values. Please confirm the newer four-colour palette replaces the earlier palette, and approve or amend the light-page / navy-header-and-footer approach.

### 4. Which headline and typography should we use?

The PDFs propose “Considered spaces. Meaningful living.” The application board also shows “Spaces with purpose.” and “Thoughtful architecture. Clearly expressed.” Which is your preferred main message? Confirm Jost for website headings and Inter for body text, or provide alternatives. The final logo typeface still needs confirmation.

### 5. Is the identity ready for website production?

Please provide the approved vector logo, separate symbol and wordmark, horizontal/stacked layouts and light/dark versions, plus font information. Confirm that the monogram, clean left upright and open spacing between the name lines are approved. Who owns final identity approval?


## Practice story & project evidence

### 6. What services can we publish?

List the services you currently offer and their boundaries, including any consultation, architectural design, interiors, planning support, technical design or delivery involvement. These examples are questions, not assumed services. Which deserve priority on the site?

### 7. What makes Domiorum distinctive?

In a few sentences, explain your design approach and why clients choose you. Supply the studio story, location, team names, roles, biographies, portraits and verified qualifications or memberships you want displayed.

### 8. How does a client work with you?

Describe your actual stages from first enquiry to completion, what happens at the first consultation, what the client receives and how responsibilities are shared. Is the initial consultation paid or free? Should fees be discussed privately or explained on the website?

### 9. Which projects should lead the portfolio?

Please nominate your strongest launch projects and identify the homepage feature. For each, provide: approved title; publishable location; project type; date and status; your exact role and scope; client brief; design response; materials or notable decisions; and images/drawings with captions and credits. Label completed, in-progress and concept work accurately.

### 10. Can the project material be published?

Supply original high-resolution files and confirm permission to publish the photography, drawings and project details. Identify photographer/collaborator credits and anything confidential. Confirm whether client names, exact addresses or interiors must be withheld.

### 11. What can establish trust?

Are there approved testimonials, awards, press mentions or professional affiliations we can use? Provide exact wording, attribution and supporting links or records. If the practice is new, what genuine project or team experience can be described with clear credits?

### 12. Who will supply and approve the content?

Who will write the first draft, collect the assets and approve the final page copy? If project photography is limited, do you want to commission it or launch with a smaller, accurately labelled portfolio?

### Image-use clarification

The identity PDF labels its architectural imagery as generated visual direction. The cards, project cover, signage and website opening view are illustrative applications. They guide the look and feel; they do not establish completed projects, office details or production-ready website assets.


## Website scope, enquiries & ownership

### 13. Can we agree the launch pages?

Approve Home, Projects, project detail pages, Studio, Services and Contact, plus privacy information and enquiry confirmation/error states. How many project pages should launch? Do you need anything else, such as journal posts, careers, downloadable profiles or multiple languages? Separate launch essentials from later additions.

### 14. How should visitors contact you?

Confirm the studio email, phone, publishable address, social links and preferred contact method. For the enquiry form, the proposed fields are name, email, project location, project type and short description; budget and timing are optional. Which fields should change? Who receives enquiries, and what response time can we realistically communicate?

### 15. Do you need additional functionality?

Is a straightforward enquiry form sufficient, or do you need booking, file uploads, CRM integration, newsletter signup or another workflow? Name existing tools and explain who will manage each. Do not add functionality solely because it is available.

### 16. What website setup already exists?

Do you have an existing website, registered domain, hosting, business email or preferred editing platform? Provide the existing URLs and identify the account owners. If replacing a site, which content and important page addresses should be retained? Access should be arranged through invitations or an agreed secure handover, without sending passwords in this brief.

### 17. Who will maintain the website?

Who should be able to add projects or edit services and team information? How often will content change? Confirm whether you need training and ongoing maintenance, and who will own the domain, hosting and website accounts.

### 18. What launch quality and measurement do you expect?

The supplied strategy proposes mobile-friendly pages, optimised images, readable text, keyboard access, a WCAG 2.2 AA target, editable projects, search-friendly page information and a tested enquiry flow with spam protection. Confirm this baseline. Do you want enquiry/visitor analytics? Who supplies or reviews the privacy information and any consent requirements for selected tools?

### 19. What are the budget and timing constraints?

What budget range is allocated to the website, and does it include copywriting, photography, logo completion, hosting and maintenance? Is there a preferred launch date or fixed event? When can the project content and approvals be available?

### 20. Who can approve the next stage?

Name one decision-maker and list any other reviewers. How will feedback be consolidated? Confirm which items above are approved now, which need revision and which are still pending.


## Assets & next-stage agreement

### Please send or assign an owner

1. Final logo package and font information.
2. Selected project list, original images/drawings, descriptions, status and credits.
3. Publication permissions and confidentiality restrictions.
4. Confirmed services, working process, studio story and team profiles.
5. Approved contact details, social links and genuine testimonials.
6. Existing website/domain information and account owners.
7. Privacy content owner, content approver and maintenance contact.

### Minimum decisions to begin page design

Confirm the audience and service area; approve the palette, headline and logo direction; agree launch pages and project count; nominate the featured project; identify the enquiry destination; and confirm the budget, target timing and decision-maker. Missing assets should have an owner and an agreed delivery date.

### Proposed next stage

Once these decisions are agreed, we can prepare the page structure, content outline, homepage design and one representative project-page design for review. Approval of that design stage can then establish the scope for responsive build, content entry, enquiry setup, testing and launch. Fees, dates and revision allowances should be agreed separately; this brief does not establish them.
