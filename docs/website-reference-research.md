# Website reference and competitor research

Reviewed 5 October 2026. These are website observations and design recommendations, not an independent verification of studios' projects, awards, statistics, prices, or business performance. Local studios are potential competitors selected for location and service overlap; the client has not supplied a confirmed competitor list.

## Recommended direction

An architectural editorial website for Dhaka residential and interior clients. Lead with a real project image and the client's provisional headline. Use ivory for most reading surfaces, navy typography and navigation, and gold sparingly for accents. Build a visible path from project discovery to consultation. Preserve Domiorum's identity rather than reproducing any reference.

## Proposed homepage sequence

1. Horizontal brand logo and compact navigation; visible consultation action.
2. One approved project photograph, provisional headline, location/service line, View Projects and consultation actions.
3. Three featured projects with varied image proportions and clearly labeled project links.
4. Short studio introduction focused on collaboration and actual expertise.
5. The six selected services, with concise scope descriptions.
6. A simple process overview, subject to client confirmation.
7. Paid consultation explanation with confirmed terms, once supplied.
8. Contact/footer with Careers, social link, and approved studio details.

Projects page: all six records; avoid filters unless the eventual categories justify them. Project page: overview image, approved location/year/area/scope, short narrative, gallery, materials where verified, and next-project link. Contact form: visible labels, select/check options for project type and stage, optional short message; only show successful submission after actual delivery.

## Implementation implications

The Next.js/TypeScript/Tailwind/Vercel plan still fits. Static project pages and optimized images are sufficient for the first phase. Use CSS transitions initially; elaborate animation libraries are not required by these references. Reserve image dimensions, support keyboard navigation, keep text legible, and accommodate reduced motion. Body type should be comfortably readable, provisionally 18px on desktop with responsive sizing. Gold should be an accent rather than small low-contrast text on ivory.

Client images and final copy remain pending; use visibly identified placeholders during review. No competitor imagery, project facts, pricing, testimonials, or achievements should be reused as Domiorum content.

## Client-identified competitors - 6 October 2026

The user identifies Shibori Design Studio and Minimal Limited as direct competitors. These take priority over the earlier researcher-selected comparison set. Zarin Nawar is confirmed by the user as Domiorum's founder and CEO.

### Shibori Design Studio

https://shiboribd.com/

Inspected the live desktop hero and accessible homepage content after the automatic browser check completed. Uses a dark interior photograph, large left-aligned sans-serif heading, contact action, social links, and oversized decorative type. Content covers process, interior/architecture/construction services, category-based portfolio, and testimonials. Many Learn More links route to portfolio; service descriptions repeat. Domiorum opportunity: retain strong image-led presentation but provide specific project narratives, differentiated service descriptions, and relevant FAQs about scope, design fees, site visits, deliverables, and consultation steps. Use clearer destination labels. Do not reuse Shibori's project counts or testimonials.

### Minimal Limited

https://www.minimallimited.com/

Inspected live desktop hero and official homepage content. Light navigation over large interior imagery, centered message, and gold Book A Meeting action. Messaging emphasizes service delivery, cost clarity, process milestones, quality assurance, after-sales support, and testimonials. A founder-led design relationship, transparent scope, and evidence from actual project stories are appropriate positioning directions. Confirm whether Zarin personally leads every project before promising direct involvement at all stages. Do not imply Domiorum offers warranties, fixed delivery timelines, or team scale without confirmation.

## Hero replacement and scroll layout repair - 6 October 2026

Revisited https://dsgninterior.se/en in the live browser: a full-screen interior photograph, oversized typography and restrained navigation. Adopted the photographic opening principle using Domiorum's supplied living room render, the approved headline and navy/ivory/gold palette. The two headline lines sit at opposing edges on desktop and stack on phones. Existing GSAP scene wipes and close-up motion continue after the opening.

Also reviewed the 21st.dev Container Scroll Animation listing at https://21st.dev/@manuarora700/components/container-scroll-animation. Its perspective-card pattern did not fit this photographic direction; no component code was copied and no additional animation dependency was added. The 21st CLI catalog lookup could not reach npm in the restricted environment, so official web listings and the live reference were used.

The live in-app browser exposed an incorrect downstream pin position: the gallery ended before its physical scroll space, leaving an empty viewport. Both animated sections now keep their travel in independent outer wrappers, with GSAP pinSpacing disabled. This keeps page geometry stable while ScrollTrigger refreshes its generated pin spacers. Checked the gallery in the live browser and added regression coverage for reload, responsive resizing, and motion toggling.
